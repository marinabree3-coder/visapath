ABOUT VISAPATH

Global guidance, with a human when it matters.

VisaPath is a Kenya-based visa consultation and application-support business designed to serve clients internationally.

Our platform combines structured visa guidance, document-readiness analysis and access to human consultants. It is designed to make complicated immigration information easier to understand and organize.

Important: VisaPath is independent of governments and immigration authorities. We do not guarantee visa approval. Final decisions are made by the relevant authority.
